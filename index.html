Ask the community ↗
Is Lokal free and open source? +

Yes. Lokal is an open-source music player. The desktop and mobile apps are maintained in separate GitHub repositories, each with its own license and development history.

Do I need an account to listen? +

You can listen to your local files on mobile without a Lokal account. Certain desktop capabilities and optional online integrations may have their own account requirements.

Can I use Lokal on a tablet or iPad? +

Yes. Lokal Mobile includes an adaptive workspace for Android tablets, foldables and iPads. Download the Android ARM64 APK or iOS IPA for a compatible device. iPad installation requires a compatible sideloading setup; device-specific behavior may vary.

Can I stream and discover music too? +

Lokal supports optional online discovery and playback sources, including integrations and add-ons. Availability depends on platform, source and your setup; no streaming catalog is hosted by Lokal.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature? +

Use the desktop issue tracker or the mobile issue tracker. Check for an existing report before opening a new one.
